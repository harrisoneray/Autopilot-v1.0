# Walking Mission Demonstrator — Requirements

Revision 0.2 · 25 October 2026 · Draft for owner review

## Objective
Demonstrate a portable mission-navigation system before hull, propulsion, and water integration. The operator carries the enclosure and follows LCD guidance as the human actuator. Requirements below are proposed acceptance criteria, not claims of implemented or verified capability.

## Hardware baseline
Raspberry Pi 4; Adafruit BNO055 9-DOF orientation sensor; HiLetgo VK172 USB GPS; SunFounder 2004 20×4 I²C LCD; physical USB button (model/interface pending selection); portable power (selection pending).

## Proposed decisions
- Mission button OFF means paused execution, not power off. Logging continues.
- Default arrival radius 10 m; default hold radius 15 m; tune after outdoor GPS characterization.
- Hold countdown pauses outside the radius and during manual pause; it does not reset.
- Loiter means remain inside a radius, not follow a circle. Indefinite loiter requires software skip/abort.
- UTC handles absolute schedules; monotonic elapsed time handles duration timers. Deadlines keep running during pause.
- GPS acquisition/reacquisition gets 60 s before Fault. Navigation progression and duration timers freeze immediately during GPS loss; recovery within the window restores the prior state. Faults require explicit recovery. Power-up never starts a mission.
- Carry the box facing forward. No inference that enclosure heading equals walking direction when carried sideways.
- Initial environment: pedestrian-accessible open sky, dry weather, no intentional water exposure.

## Planner and transfer decision
Preferred candidate: QGroundControl. Planner, protocol, and transfer transport remain an interface-selection gate. The intended workflow is plan on desktop → transfer to Pi → validate → acknowledge accepted mission → execute without desktop connectivity. A manual hand-edited file alone does not satisfy MIS-01. Direct MAVLink upload versus planner-export/file import must be selected after a compatibility check; do not imply that the current Pi software is already an autopilot endpoint. Unsupported commands must be rejected rather than silently approximated.

## Requirements and verification
All requirements: Proposed / Not yet verified. Numerical values are initial engineering targets, not measured sensor performance.

| ID | Function | Proposed requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIS-01 | Mission transfer | The system shall accept an ordered mission authored in desktop planning software and transferred to the Pi before execution. The uploaded mission shall persist across restart. | Import a three-waypoint mission; restart and compare coordinates, order, radii, and timing fields. |
| MIS-02 | Mission validation | The system shall reject malformed coordinates, unsupported commands, invalid radii, and inconsistent times with an actionable error. A failed upload shall preserve the previous valid mission. Mission replacement shall be blocked during Active or Paused execution. | Attempt invalid uploads and an upload during execution; confirm rejection and retained mission. |
| MIS-03 | Supported commands | The first release shall support waypoint transit, hold for a duration, remain inside a radius indefinitely, wait until a UTC time, and an optional arrive-by UTC deadline. Circular-track loiter is deferred. | Execute one mission containing each supported behavior; reject an unsupported command. |
| MIS-04 | Waypoint arrival | Default waypoint acceptance radius shall be 10 m, configurable per waypoint. Arrival requires three consecutive valid position samples inside the radius, spanning at least 2 s. | Replay boundary-crossing data; confirm no early arrival and exactly one advance. |
| MIS-05 | Timed hold | Default hold radius shall be 15 m. Hold time shall accumulate only while Active with valid position inside the radius; time outside shall pause, not reset, the countdown. A completed hold shall advance once. | Run a 60 s hold with an excursion and a manual pause; compare counted time to the event log. |
| MIS-06 | Indefinite loiter | Radius loiter shall instruct HOLD inside the assigned radius and return-to-center guidance outside it. It shall remain on that item until an explicit software skip or abort; button toggling shall not skip it. | Cross the boundary, pause/resume, then explicitly skip; verify the item does not complete on its own. |
| MIS-07 | Absolute timing | Absolute times shall use UTC and require a synchronized clock. Wait-until shall complete only at/after its scheduled time while inside the radius with valid position and Active execution. Arrive-by shall flag a missed deadline and continue guidance without skipping the target. | Simulate early/late arrival, clock unavailability, and pause spanning the scheduled time. |
| NAV-01 | Position validity | Position shall require a receiver-reported valid fix and a sample no older than 3 s. Fix type, sample age, satellites, and HDOP shall be logged when available. Validity shall not be presented as a guarantee of positional accuracy. | Feed valid, invalid, and stale data; verify status, logs, and progression gating. |
| NAV-02 | Heading reference | The enclosure shall have a marked forward axis. BNO055 mounting orientation and heading offset shall be recorded. Navigation shall use true-north bearings; magnetic heading shall receive a documented local declination correction. | Check orientation and turning sign against known directions; record calibration and correction settings. |
| NAV-03 | Heading readiness | Relative turn guidance shall require a fresh heading no older than 1 s and BNO055 system and magnetometer calibration levels of 3. These are readiness gates, not an accuracy certification. Loss of readiness shall suppress relative turns and trigger a navigation fault. | Inject stale and uncalibrated heading; confirm suppression and fault handling. |
| NAV-04 | Guidance | While navigating, the system shall show distance and true bearing to the target and shortest signed heading error. Default cues: MOVE AHEAD within ±15°, TURN LEFT/RIGHT beyond that, and TURN AROUND at absolute error ≥150°. | Replay headings around 0°/360° and cue thresholds; verify correct sign and wraparound. |
| NAV-05 | Update performance | Position ingestion and navigation outputs shall operate at ≥1 Hz with a supported receiver configuration. Displayed guidance shall update within 1 s of receipt of a new valid position sample. Heading shall be sampled at ≥10 Hz. | Measure timestamps during a 10 min run; report achieved rates and latency. |
| CTL-01 | Startup and readiness | Every startup shall be nonexecuting. Ready shall require a validated mission, valid position, ready heading, connected button, writable log, and synchronized clock if absolute timing is used. | Restart with each prerequisite missing; verify activation is blocked with a reason. |
| CTL-02 | USB mission button | One press shall activate Ready or resume Paused; one press while Active shall pause. Only press edges shall count, with a 300 ms debounce and release required before another action. A held button shall generate one action. | Test bounce, rapid presses, a held press, and key-repeat input. |
| CTL-03 | Pause and resume | Paused execution shall freeze waypoint progression and duration timers while position and fault logging continue. Resume shall retain the current mission item and remaining hold duration. Absolute UTC deadlines shall not shift. | Pause during transit and hold, move while paused, then resume; inspect index and timer. |
| CTL-04 | Fault response | At startup, GPS shall receive a 60 s acquisition window from receiver initialization before an acquisition-timeout Fault. During execution, invalid or stale position shall immediately inhibit waypoint progression, movement instructions, and duration timers and display ACQUIRING GPS with a countdown. A monotonic 60 s reacquisition window shall start when position becomes invalid/stale. Valid position recovered before expiry shall clear the GPS warning and restore the prior execution state; manual pause shall remain paused. Continuous loss for 60 s shall enter Fault. Heading faults, button disconnect, or log-write failure shall enter Fault within 1 s of detection. Recovery from Fault shall require healthy prerequisites and an explicit button press; it shall never auto-resume. | Test initial acquisition and mid-mission GPS loss: recovery at 59 s shall avoid Fault; continuous loss at 60 s shall Fault. Verify frozen progression/timers, suppressed movement cues, countdown, preserved manual pause, and explicit recovery after Fault. Test other faults separately. |
| CTL-05 | Completion and abort | Final-item completion shall enter Complete and require explicit software reset before replay. Abort shall end the run and enter Idle; it shall require software action rather than an overloaded button gesture. Restart shall never resume a previous run automatically. | Complete, abort, and power-cycle missions; confirm no accidental replay. |
| HMI-01 | LCD navigation page | The 20×4 LCD shall show mission state, active item/total, distance, movement cue, and navigation health on its default page without scrolling critical instructions. Position and hold time shall be accessible on a secondary page. | Review all states on the physical LCD, including long distances, large item counts, and faults. |
| HMI-02 | LCD controls and units | The mission button shall control execution only. A software setting shall select LCD pages. Distance shall use meters, heading degrees true, position WGS84 decimal degrees, and timers seconds or mm:ss; precision shall not imply accuracy. | Inspect page selection, units, truncation, and state feedback. |
| LOG-01 | Run identity and provenance | Every activation from Ready shall create a unique run ID recording the exact mission snapshot/hash, configuration, software version, start time, and timestamp synchronization state. Resume shall retain that run ID. | Compare two runs and a pause/resume cycle; reproduce each run’s inputs. |
| LOG-02 | Telemetry and events | Each run shall log position and validity at ≥1 Hz when available, heading/calibration, current item, distance/bearing/error, state, and hold timer. State changes, button events, arrivals, timing violations, and faults shall carry timestamps. | Reconstruct a completed mission and an interrupted run from logs alone. |
| LOG-03 | Persistence | Telemetry shall flush at least every 2 s; transitions shall flush immediately. Orderly stop shall close the log. Sudden power loss may lose buffered data; the retained log shall remain readable. | Perform orderly stop and controlled abrupt-power tests; measure retained data and record any filesystem effects. |
| INT-01 | Portable integration | Pi 4, VK172, BNO055, LCD, and button interfaces, supply voltages, addresses/device identities, wiring, and mounting shall be documented. USB devices shall be identified persistently rather than by enumeration order. | Inspect interface record and reconnect devices in a different order. |
| INT-02 | Carried operating condition | The box shall be carried with its marked forward axis aligned with the operator’s forward direction. The assembled system shall run for 60 min on portable power without reboot, sensor interruption, or mounting movement under normal walking. | Conduct a 60 min outdoor run and inspect logs, connectors, and mounting. |
| VER-01 | Acceptance demonstration | A walking acceptance mission shall include ≥3 waypoints, a 60 s hold, a wait-until item, an arrive-by condition, indefinite radius loiter with explicit skip, pause/resume, and an induced navigation fault. All outcomes shall be traceable to run logs. | Execute the documented route and replay deterministic fault/timing cases where field timing is impractical. |

## State behavior
Idle: no executable mission. Ready: prerequisites met. Active: execute current item. Paused: retain progress; log continues. Fault: execution inhibited with reason. Complete: terminal until reset. Abort ends the run and returns to Idle. Acknowledging a recovered fault resumes the interrupted item only after all readiness checks pass. Faults detected while Paused also inhibit resume. GPS acquisition/reacquisition is a timed navigation substate: it inhibits activation or progression immediately but becomes Fault only after 60 s of continuous invalid/stale position. Reacquisition before timeout restores the prior state without overriding a manual pause. Absolute UTC deadlines continue during GPS acquisition/reacquisition.

## Acceptance evidence
1. Stationary open-sky GPS characterization for 10 min; record scatter, sample age, fix quality, and update rate. Validate or revise radii before route acceptance.
2. Heading checks after final enclosure assembly; document calibration, mounting axis, declination, and observed error against an independent reference.
3. Deterministic replay verifies boundary hysteresis, timers, heading wraparound, stale data, and state transitions.
4. Walking route demonstrates end-to-end upload, guidance, holds, operator controls, faults, and log reconstruction. Keep route truth/reference and actual results with the run ID.
5. Repeat complete route twice with no unintended waypoint skips or automatic resumes. Record discrepancies; do not mark requirements verified until evidence exists.

## Review priorities / open interfaces
Confirm the 10 m / 15 m radii and 60 min endurance target after characterization. Select USB button and power source. Resolve planner command/transport compatibility. GPS update rate and BNO055 heading performance remain to be measured. Confirm LCD electrical compatibility and BNO055 interface behavior before final mounting.
